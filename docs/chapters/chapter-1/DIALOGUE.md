# Chapter 1 — Dialogue Script

## Havenfall Gate
**Mara:** Stop there.

**Wanderer:** ...

**Mara:** Havenfall isn't a road stop.

**Wanderer:** I wasn't looking for it.

**Mara:** Then you're either lost or lying.

If the player saw the light:

**Wanderer:** I saw the light.

**Mara:** From the north?

**Wanderer:** Yes.

**Mara:** Then get inside.

If the player does not remember:

**Wanderer:** I don't remember.

**Mara:** Convenient.

**Wanderer:** I remember waking by the river.

**Mara:** And the light?

**Wanderer:** Nothing.

**Mara:** Then we're both going to have a long day.

If the player denies knowing:

**Wanderer:** What light?

**Mara:** ...

**Mara:** You really don't know.

**Wanderer:** Know what?

**Mara:** Get inside.

## Chapel of Ash
**Cael:** Your hand.

**Wanderer:** What about it?

**Cael:** Where did you get that mark?

**Wanderer:** I've had it as long as I can remember.

**Cael:** No.

Pause.

**Cael:** You haven't.

**Wanderer:** You know what it is.

**Cael:** I know what it means.

**Wanderer:** Then tell me.

**Cael:** Not here.

The chapel door closes.

## Blue Fire
**Mara:** Don't touch it.

**Wanderer:** It's fire.

**Mara:** Fire doesn't burn blue.

**Voice:** Not yet.

Mara freezes.

**Mara:** You heard that.

**Wanderer:** Yes.

**Mara:** Good.

**Mara:** I was hoping I was losing my mind.

## Elias
**Elias:** Don't move.

**Wanderer:** You said that like you expected me.

**Elias:** I didn't.

**Wanderer:** Then why are you here?

**Elias:** I followed a map.

**Wanderer:** Your map?

**Elias:** No.

**Elias:** It drew itself.

## Hollow Knight
**Wanderer:** Who are you?

The Knight raises its sword.

The Wanderer's scar glows.

The Knight kneels.

**Hollow Knight:** You are late.

The Knight disappears.

## Heart Chamber
**Crystal:** Remember.

**Wanderer:** Remember what?

**Crystal:** Yourself.

## Ending
**Elias:** Mara.

**Mara:** What?

**Elias:** Look.

**Mara:** That's not a star.

**Elias:** Then what is it?

**Mara:** I don't know.

**Mara:** Get everyone inside.

## Post-credit
**Cael:** The first one has awakened.

**Unknown Voice:** No.

**Unknown Voice:** The first one has returned.
