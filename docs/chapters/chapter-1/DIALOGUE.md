# Chapter 1 — Dialogue Script

## Havenfall Gate

Mara: Stop there.

Wanderer: ...

Mara: Havenfall isn't a road stop.

Wanderer: I wasn't looking for it.

Mara: Then you're either lost or lying.

Choice — saw the light:
Wanderer: I saw the light.
Mara: From the north?
Wanderer: Yes.
Mara: Then get inside.

Choice — memory missing:
Wanderer: I don't remember.
Mara: Convenient.
Wanderer: I remember waking by the river.
Mara: And the light?
Wanderer: Nothing.
Mara: Then we're both going to have a long day.

Choice — deny:
Wanderer: What light?
Mara: ...
Mara: You really don't know.
Wanderer: Know what?
Mara: Get inside.

## Chapel of Ash
Cael: Your hand.
Wanderer: What about it?
Cael: Where did you get that mark?
Wanderer: I've had it as long as I can remember.
Cael: No.
Cael: You haven't.
Wanderer: You know what it is.
Cael: I know what it means.
Wanderer: Then tell me.
Cael: Not here.

## Blue Fire
Mara: Don't touch it.
Wanderer: It's fire.
Mara: Fire doesn't burn blue.
Voice: Not yet.
Mara: You heard that.
Wanderer: Yes.
Mara: Good. I was hoping I was losing my mind.

## Elias
Elias: Don't move.
Wanderer: You said that like you expected me.
Elias: I didn't.
Wanderer: Then why are you here?
Elias: I followed a map.
Wanderer: Your map?
Elias: No. It drew itself.

## Hollow Knight
Wanderer: Who are you?
The Knight raises its sword. The Wanderer's scar glows. The Knight kneels.
Hollow Knight: You are late.
The Knight disappears.

## Heart Chamber
Crystal: Remember.
Wanderer: Remember what?
Crystal: Yourself.

## Ending
Elias: Mara.
Mara: What?
Elias: Look.
Mara: That's not a star.
Elias: Then what is it?
Mara: I don't know. Get everyone inside.

## Post-credit
Cael: The first one has awakened.
Unknown Voice: No.
Unknown Voice: The first one has returned.


# Ambient Conversation Library

## Bakery

Elias: Doesn't it?
Bram: No. It starts when everyone forgets breakfast.
Elias: That's your theory?
Bram: It's the only theory that has survived three winters.

## Blacksmith

Villager: I was only looking.

## Guard Shift
Guard One: Did you hear that?
Guard Two: No.
Guard One: Exactly.
Guard Two: That's not how hearing works.
Guard One: I know what I heard.

## Children

## River
