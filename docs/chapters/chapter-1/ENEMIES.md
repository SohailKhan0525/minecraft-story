# Chapter 1 — Enemy Design

## Hollowed Wolf
Fast early-game enemy. Circles the player, retreats when injured, and attacks in pairs.

## Ashbound Skeleton
Ancient undead using stone swords. Occasionally blocks and retreats toward relic chambers.

## Silent Stalker
Rare forest enemy. Appears at the edge of vision, disappears when approached directly, and attacks after story thresholds.

## Stone Guardian
Slow observatory construct with heavy attacks and frontal armor. Vulnerable from behind.

## Observatory Warden
Elite construct that creates temporary stone barriers and summons one Stone Guardian.

## The Warden of the Deep
Phase 1 — Sentinel: slow sweeps, ground shock, guardian summons; weak at rear crystal node.

Phase 2 — Fracture: arena partially collapses; crystal beams and platform-breaking pulses; player activates three crystal anchors.

Phase 3 — Memory: boss copies the player's attack pattern. The player must stop attacking and interact with the heart crystal to end the fight.

The combat communicates the chapter's theme: the player is recovering a buried truth, not simply defeating an enemy.
