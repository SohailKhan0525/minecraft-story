# Chapter 1 — Character Bible

## The Wanderer
The player character. Capable and observant, but missing critical memories. Player dialogue choices establish personality while the central identity mystery remains authored.

## Mara Vale
Havenfall's warden. Direct, disciplined, protective, and skeptical until evidence forces her to accept the supernatural. Her younger brother vanished in the Silent Forest.

## Elias Venn
Seventeen-year-old apprentice mapmaker. Curious and quick-talking when frightened. He discovers the impossible map and becomes the bridge between Havenfall and the buried observatory.

## Brother Cael
Keeper of the Chapel of Ash. Gentle in public and terrified in private. He recognizes the Wanderer's scar and has spent decades guarding the secret behind it.

## Sera Voss
Survivor of a vanished expedition. Competent, exhausted, and suspicious. The player's first major choice determines whether she becomes a trusted ally.

## The Hollow Knight
A silent armored guardian. It recognizes the Wanderer and is connected to the ancient order beneath Havenfall.

## The Warden of the Deep
An ancient guardian executing an old command. It is not motivated by hatred; it is protecting the Heart Chamber.
