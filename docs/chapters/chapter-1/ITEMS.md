# Chapter 1 — Story Items

## Warden's Iron Sword
An iron sword issued by Mara. Flavor: A practical weapon for practical problems.

## Ash Lens
Ancient optical device that reveals hidden markings and observatory mechanisms.

## Star-Iron Shard
Fragment of the fallen star that powers ancient mechanisms.

## Warden Seal
Ancient insignia used to open the final observatory gate.

## Black Crystal
A story object in the Heart Chamber. It is one of twelve keys and reacts to the Wanderer's scar.

## Scar of the Twelve
Persistent narrative state. Its appearance changes after the Heart Chamber choice. It is cosmetic and narrative rather than a generic stat bonus.
