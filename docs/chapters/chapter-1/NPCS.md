# Chapter 1 — NPC Roster

## Main NPCs

### Mara Vale
Role: Warden.
Story function: protagonist gatekeeper, protector, emotional anchor.

### Elias Venn
Role: apprentice mapmaker.
Story function: mystery guide and comic relief.

### Brother Cael
Role: chapel keeper.
Story function: lore keeper and hidden-identity thread.

### Sera Voss
Role: expedition survivor.
Story function: first major choice and future companion.

## Havenfall recurring NPCs

### Bram the Baker
Warm, dramatic, and convinced every problem can be solved with bread.

Sample:
"People think the end of the world starts with fire."
Pause.
"Wrong."
"The end starts when everyone forgets breakfast."

### Nessa the Blacksmith
Blunt, practical, and constantly annoyed by people asking if a sword is sharp.

Sample:
"Is it sharp?"
"Yes."
"How sharp?"
"Sharp enough to answer the question."

### Pip and Toma
Two young siblings who turn rumors into increasingly ridiculous stories.

Pip:
"I saw the star fall."
Toma:
"You saw a chicken fall."
Pip:
"It was a very bright chicken."
Toma:
"It was on fire."
Pip:
"Exactly."

### Old Renn
Retired guard. Tells stories that sound absurd until one of them turns out to be true.

### Lio the Fisher
Quiet observer. Provides atmospheric comments about the river and changing wildlife.

## NPC behavior

NPCs have schedules rather than standing permanently beside quest markers.

Examples:
- Bram opens the bakery at dawn.
- Nessa works the forge during daylight.
- Guards change shifts.
- Children disappear indoors after the blue-fire event.
- Villagers gather near the chapel during the silent event.
- After Elias disappears, his usual map table is visibly empty.

The player should be able to notice these changes without a UI notification.
