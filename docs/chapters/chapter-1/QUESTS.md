# Chapter 1 — Quest Design

## A Bell Before Breakfast
Wake beside the river, follow the bell, reach Havenfall, meet Mara, and enter the village.
Reward: Warden's Iron Sword, bread, Havenfall access.

## Blue Fire
Investigate the northern edge, inspect the blue flame, hear the voice, return to Mara.
Reward: northern road access; heard_the_voice.

## Find Elias
Find Elias's map, follow the impossible marker, reach the forest, discover the staircase.
Reward: Elias trust; observatory access.

## Beneath the Roots
Descend, activate three ancient mechanisms, witness visions, find Elias, reach the choice chamber.
Reward: Ash Lens, Star-Iron Shard, first identity clue.

## The Door Beneath the World
Recover the Warden Seal, solve the flooded archive, open the sealed gate, encounter the Hollow Knight.
Reward: Heart Chamber access.

## The Hollow Knight
A cinematic confrontation rather than a normal combat encounter. The Knight cannot be meaningfully damaged.
Reward: hollow_knight_seen.

## The Heart of the Observatory
Enter the Heart Chamber, awaken the crystal, survive three boss phases, and make the final choice.
Reward: Chapter 1 completion and persistent ending choice.

## The Night Is Not Over
Return to Havenfall, speak with Mara and Elias, reveal four stars, and unlock Chapter 2.
Reward: chapter_1_complete; chapter_2_unlocked.
