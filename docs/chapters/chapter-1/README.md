## Chapter goal

Create a complete, playable first chapter that introduces the world, main characters, core gameplay loop, and central mystery.

## Planned structure

1. Opening
2. Player introduction
3. First important NPC
4. First quest
5. Exploration
6. First meaningful choice
7. Escalation
8. Dungeon / major encounter
9. Boss or climax
10. Chapter ending / hook for Chapter 2

## Design questions

- Who is the player?
- What is the central mystery?
- What is the first location?
- Who is the first major NPC?
- What does the player want?
- What threatens the world?
- What is the chapter's emotional payoff?
- What choice or discovery should make players want Chapter 2?
