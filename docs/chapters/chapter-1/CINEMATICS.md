# Chapter 1 — Cinematic Direction

The game should feel like a film the player can walk through.

## Opening
1. Black screen.
2. Forest canopy.
3. Wolf running.
4. Birds leaving trees.
5. Wolf stops.
6. Ambient audio cuts.
7. Clouds part.
8. One star.
9. Star accelerates.
10. Impact flash.
11. Black.
12. Heartbeat.
13. Title.

## Havenfall
Establish the village with a wide shot before the player enters. Follow the player from behind. When Mara appears, settle the camera at eye level. Do not frame the player as a hero yet.

## Silent Forest
Use foreground trunks, fog, silhouettes, distant movement, and empty spaces where wildlife should be. The absence of sound is the primary horror device.

## Hollow Knight
No particle explosion. No roar. No dramatic music. The Knight is simply standing where the player was not looking.

## Heart Chamber
Reveal the room in beats: beauty, eleven skeletons, empty twelfth seat, black crystal, scar reaction, boss awakening.

## Ending
Reveal the stars one at a time with long pauses. One. Two. Three. Four. Then the mountain opens.

## Music motifs
Wanderer: sparse piano and strings.
Havenfall: warm acoustic motif.
Silent Forest: low drones and distant metallic tones.
Observatory: choir, glass tones, slow percussion.
Hollow Knight: near silence with one low sustained note.

Silence is an authored effect during the blue-fire whisper, Silent Forest arrival, Hollow Knight appearance, crystal awakening, and final star reveal.
